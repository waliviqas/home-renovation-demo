The Problem
Renovation is a black box
01

Is the project even allowed?

Nobody knows what is feasible on their own house. Does this renovation comply with county code? Homeowners can't answer the one question that decides everything, so they guess, or they stall.

02

Is the quote a real number?

Will $10,000 actually buy the two-bedroom addition you want? With no independent cost model, a homeowner cannot tell a fair bid from a fantasy, and bids for the same job swing 2–3×.

03

You can't see it until it's built

A floor plan is abstract and renderings cost money. Homeowners commit six figures without ever seeing what the house actually becomes.
