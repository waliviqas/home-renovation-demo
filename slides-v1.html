The Problem

Renovation is a black box

01

Nobody knows what's feasible

Is that wall load-bearing? Is the foundation slab or raised? Homeowners can't answer the questions that decide everything — so they guess, or stall.

02

Quotes are wild guesses

Contractor bids for the same job swing 2–3×. Without an independent feasibility baseline, homeowners negotiate blind.

03

Permits are county chaos

Every Bay Area county plays by different rules. What's a weekend project in one city is a 6-month permit saga in the next.
